# SkillFlow

SkillFlow is a student service marketplace that helps Senior High School students turn their skills into credible project experience and career evidence while connecting with people who need their services.

## Language

**SkillFlow**:
The marketplace and career-development product described by the research study.
_Avoid_: SkillHub

**Student Designer**:
A Senior High School student who presents skills, offers services, completes client work, and builds career evidence through SkillFlow.
_Avoid_: Seller, freelancer, service provider

**Client**:
A person or organization seeking work from a Student Designer.
_Avoid_: Buyer, customer, account

**Service Listing**:
A Student Designer's published offering, including its scope, price, delivery expectation, and revision terms.
_Avoid_: Gig, product

**Project Post**:
A Client's description of work for which Student Designers may submit proposals.
_Avoid_: Job listing, service request

**Proposal**:
A Student Designer's offer to complete a Project Post for a stated amount and delivery time.
_Avoid_: Bid, application

**Project Booking**:
The shared engagement between a Client and Student Designer, originating from either a requested Service Listing or an accepted Proposal.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction, contract

**Digital Portfolio**:
A Student Designer's collection of work samples and completed-project evidence used to demonstrate skills and accomplishments.
_Avoid_: Gallery, résumé

**Student Verification**:
The product's indication that a Student Designer's student identity has been reviewed for the academic demonstration.
_Avoid_: Identity verification, KYC

**AI Project Mentor**:
The guidance feature that gives Student Designers suggestions for improving project ideas, outputs, designs, and portfolios before submission. It does not guarantee work quality or replace professional advice.
_Avoid_: AI Project Monitor, chatbot

**Career Readiness Score**:
A transparent indicator of a Student Designer's career preparation based on profile, verification, portfolio, completed projects, ratings, and certifications.
_Avoid_: Employability rating, performance score

**Demo Wallet**:
The demonstration-only view of simulated project holds, releases, and earnings.
_Avoid_: Payment method, bank account, escrow wallet
